Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Kaj System ("we", "us") builds software for clients. This page explains what personal information this website collects and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Contact form. When you send us a message we receive the name, email address, phone number, project details and message you typed. We use them only to reply to you and prepare a proposal.
  • Preferences. Your language and light/dark choice are stored in your own browser (local storage) so the site remembers them. They never leave your device.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider records standard technical data (IP address, browser type, pages requested) to keep the site secure and available.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your details with third parties for marketing.
  • We do not use advertising trackers or social-media pixels on this site.

Third-party services

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. If the contact form is configured to use a form service (such as Formspree), your message passes through that provider's servers under its own privacy policy. Links to WhatsApp or a booking tool open those services directly.

Retention and your rights

We keep project enquiries for as long as needed to discuss and deliver your project, then delete them. You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by writing to the email address on our contact page.
