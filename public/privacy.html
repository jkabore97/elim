Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

ELIM is a church community app. Verified church accounts publish content — text, photos, audio, video, and documents — and members follow along, like, and comment. This page explains what information ELIM collects and how it's used.

Information we collect

  • Account information: email address and display name, required to create an account.
  • Optional profile information: profile picture, church name, country, city, and phone number — all editable by you at any time, and none of it required.
  • Content you create: posts (for church accounts), comments, and likes.
  • Google account information (only if you use "Continue with Google" to sign in): your name, email, and profile photo, as shared by Google. We don't receive your Google password or any other Google account data.

We do not collect payment information, precise location data, or health information.

How information is used

  • To create and secure your account
  • To display your profile and posts to other members of the app
  • To let church accounts publish content and members engage with it (likes, comments)

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising purposes.

Data storage and security

ELIM's data is stored using Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore Database, and Cloud Storage). Access to your data is controlled by server-side security rules — for example, only you can edit your own profile, and only the church that published a post can edit or delete it.

Your choices

  • You can edit or remove your profile picture, church, country, city, and phone number at any time from the Profile tab.
  • You can delete any post or comment you've created.
  • To request deletion of your account entirely, contact us using the information below.

Children's privacy

ELIM is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to the app administrator through the church listed in the app.
